From our office near Hurontario and the 401 to the Hamilton Mountain is about an hour on a good day. We don't take jobs further out than that, because a lead who's stuck on the QEW can't be on your site.

Mississauga

Home base

Most of our work is within twenty minutes of the office. A lot of it is basements in Meadowvale and Erin Mills townhouses and semis, many of them becoming legal second suites, plus flooring swaps in older Streetsville and Port Credit homes. Lorne Park kitchens tend to be the bigger jobs.

Oakville

Glen Abbey and Joshua Creek houses from the eighties and nineties are due for their first real kitchen and ensuite, and that's most of what we do here. Engineered hardwood through the main floor usually follows. Closer to the lake in Bronte, it's more often the backyard.

Burlington

Hamilton

Westdale, Dundas and the older streets below the escarpment have century homes on stone or block foundations that let water in. Waterproofing comes before finishing there, and some basements need underpinning to get a ceiling you can stand under. Up on the Mountain, in Ancaster and Stoney Creek, it's more kitchens, baths and patios.
