Why TrustBuildGTA

Why homeowners hire a general contractor they can check up on

The name is the promise, so here's what it means in practice. It's also what you should ask any contractor before you sign.

  • Written fixed-price quotes

    Every line priced, materials named, HST shown separately. No vague allowances that quietly double. If you add something halfway through, you get a written change order with a price first.

  • One point of contact

    Your project lead is on site, knows your house and answers their phone. Nobody hands you off to a coordinator after the contract is signed. The quote also carries a completion date, and if a delay is ours, you hear it from us the day we know.

  • Permits handled

    Where the Ontario Building Code calls for a permit, we apply for it, book the inspections and are on site when the inspector comes. Second suites, structural walls and attached decks are all permit jobs.

  • Site cleaned daily

    Floor protection goes down on day one. Zippered dust walls go up around the work area. The site gets swept and vacuumed before we leave each day, because you still live there.

Licensed and insured with liability coverage and WSIB. Certificates are available on request before you sign.
